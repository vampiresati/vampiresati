Using AI to speed up construction PDF review

Construction plansheets and specification PDFs can contain useful structure, but that structure is often locked inside large documents. A practical automation workflow starts by splitting the PDF into pages, converting pages to images and extracting the information that reviewers need.

Workflow

  • Upload plansheet PDFs and split them into individual pages.
  • Convert each page into an image for visual analysis.
  • Use the first-page selection to identify title and drawing number patterns.
  • Predict drawing titles and numbers on following pages.
  • Predict specification codes from specification documents.

Result

This type of workflow helps reduce repetitive review time and creates a structured starting point for reporting, searching and downstream project coordination.
